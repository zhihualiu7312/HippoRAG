QG-PPR — Patent Summary
Query-Conditioned Gated PPR for Multi-Granular Graph Retrieval
KEY IDEA Learn a query-specific PPR policy that controls “Where to Start” + “Where to Propagate”.
KEY CLAIMS 1. Query-conditioned relevance injection 2. Query-conditioned propagation gating 3. Joint gated PPR on entity/passage/paper graph 4. Differentiable training of the policy
DISTINCTION Baseline: fixed / heuristic diffusion QG-PPR: query-specific routing, gates, and diffusion control
Core mechanism
Query → Policy → relevance injection → gated propagation → PPR → passage ranking
Policy predicts granularity routing, type-specific gate parameters, and diffusion control.
Target effect: suppress structural noise while retaining useful multi-hop evidence.
Baseline limitation
Traditional PPR-based retrieval can start from relevant seeds but propagates through a largely query-independent graph diffusion process.
High-degree generic nodes may absorb relevance mass and create noise cascades.
QG-PPR makes both initialization and propagation query-conditioned.

6. Innovation I — Query-Conditioned Relevance Injection
Goal: WHERE TO START?
Each node receives a query-node similarity score.
Node-type-specific sigmoid gates convert relevance into a soft filter.
Granularity routing weights adjust the contribution of entity, passage and paper nodes.
Result: every query obtains its own relevance distribution over the graph.
s(q,v) = cos(q_emb, e_v)
g_v(q) = σ[k_T(q)(s(q,v) − τ_T(q))]
r_q(v) = r(v) · w_T(q) · g_v(q)
Different Query → Different Initial Relevance Landscape

7. Innovation II — Query-Conditioned Propagation
Goal: WHERE TO PROPAGATE?
Initial relevance alone cannot prevent later noise cascade.
During each propagation step, node relevance is modulated by the query-conditioned gate.
Relevant nodes retain propagation strength; low-relevance nodes are continuously attenuated.
p^(t+1) = d_q r_q + (1−d_q) M (p^(t) ⊙ g_q)

One lightweight policy generates query-specific parameters for all graph granularities.
Softmax routing emphasizes the most useful granularity; independent sigmoid parameters control selectivity.
The design replaces manually tuned, query-independent weights with a data-driven retrieval policy.

M2 Conditioned Propagation
M3 / QG-PPR Joint
M1 learns WHERE TO START: query-specific personalization / relevance injection.
M2 learns WHERE TO PROPAGATE: query-specific gating during diffusion.
M3 combines both mechanisms and therefore controls the complete retrieval trajectory.
Core claim: QG-PPR jointly conditions relevance initialization and graph propagation on the query.
QG-PPR = Query-Conditioned Injection + Query-Conditioned Propagation

All query-conditioned parameters remain connected to the computational graph.

14. Summary of the Invention
QG-PPR makes PPR query-conditioned at both the starting point and the propagation process.
1. Query-conditioned relevance injection determines WHERE TO START.
2. Query-conditioned gating determines WHERE TO PROPAGATE.
3. Multi-granular routing adapts entity / passage / paper importance to the query.
4. Differentiable PPR enables the retrieval policy to be learned directly from ranking supervision.
5. The combined mechanism reduces noise propagation while preserving useful multi-hop evidence.
